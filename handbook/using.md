# Using the handbook

The handbook collects the user manuals of all installed extensions. Open it
from **Support › Handbook**.

## Overview

The start page shows one card per extension with its manuals, grouped by
section. Problems with a manual are listed at the top.

## Contents

The table of contents sits beside every manual. Each extension is a group
that opens and closes with a click on its name. The group of the manual you
are reading is open. Its first entry, "Overview", leads to the extension's
start page if it has one. Small headings divide longer manuals into
sections.

## Reading a manual

A path above the manual shows where you are: Handbook › extension › section.
"On this page" lists the sections of the manual. On very wide screens the
list sits to the right of the text and stays visible while you scroll;
otherwise it sits above the text. On narrow screens the table of contents
comes first. At the bottom, "Previous page" and "Next page" follow the order
of the table of contents.

## Searching

The search field is on every handbook page. Search finds every section that
contains the term, regardless of upper and lower case. Hits are grouped by
manual; each manual shows its first five sections, each with the first line
that contains the term. Every hit leads straight to its section.

## Links on other screens

When a manual belongs to a particular CiviCRM screen, a "Handbook: …" link
appears at the top of that screen. How to set this up is described in
[Writing manuals](writing-manuals.md#linking-pages).

## Languages

The handbook shows each manual in your CiviCRM language if the extension
ships a translation, and in its default language otherwise.
